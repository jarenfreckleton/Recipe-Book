Crack Breakfast Casserole

350°F175°C oven
9×13 inBaking dish
40–45 minBake

Ingredients tap to check off

Batch size

Directions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9×13-inch baking dish.

  2. Spread the hash browns evenly in the prepared baking dish.

  3. Layer the cooked sausage and crumbled bacon over the hash browns.

  4. Sprinkle 1 1/2 cups of the shredded cheddar cheese on top.

  5. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, and ranch dressing mix until well combined. Pour this mixture evenly over the casserole.

  6. Top with the remaining 1/2 cup of cheddar cheese.

  7. Bake uncovered until the casserole is set and the cheese is melted and bubbly. 40–45 min

  8. Remove from the oven, let cool slightly, and serve warm.

Homemade Pizza Dough

105°FWarm water
5 minYeast proof
10 min–1 hrOptional rise

Ingredients tap to check off

Batch size

Make the dough

  1. Combine the warm water, yeast, and sugar in a large mixing bowl and stir to combine.

  2. Let the mixture sit until it becomes frothy and bubbles form. 5 min

  3. Gently stir in the olive oil.

  4. Add 2 cups of flour and the salt. Mix with a spatula until a ball begins to form (the dough will still be slightly sticky). Add more flour as needed to form a dough ball.

  5. Transfer to a floured surface and knead into a smooth dough, adding up to 1/2 cup extra flour if needed.

  6. Optional. Cover the bowl with a damp tea towel and let the dough rise. 10 min–1 hr

Tropical Hawaiian Chicken on a Sheet Pan

400°F200°C oven
25–30 minRoast
165°F75°C chicken

Ingredients tap to check off

Batch size

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).

  2. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, soy sauce, honey, garlic powder, ground ginger, chili flakes, salt, and pepper.

  3. Place the chicken breasts on a sheet pan. Drizzle the marinade over the chicken and turn to coat evenly.

  4. Add the pineapple chunks, red bell pepper, and onion slices to the pan around the chicken.

  5. Roast until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C) and the vegetables are tender. 25–30 min

  6. Garnish with fresh cilantro before serving.

  7. Serve the chicken with the roasted vegetables for a complete, easy meal.

Carmel Popcorn

2 bagsPopcorn
22Marshmallows
MicrowaveCaramel

Ingredients tap to check off

Batch size

Directions

  1. Strain the 2 bags of popcorn so the seeds fall to the bottom. Discard the seeds.

  2. For the caramel, combine 1 cup brown sugar, 1 cup butter, and 22 marshmallows.

  3. Microwave, stir, and repeat until it is a creamy caramel sauce.

  4. Pour over the popcorn with no seeds and enjoy.
