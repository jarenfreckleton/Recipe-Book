Crack Breakfast Casserole

350°F175°C oven
9×13 inBaking dish
40–45 minBake

Ingredients tap to check off

Batch size

Directions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9×13-inch baking dish.

  2. Spread the hash browns evenly in the prepared baking dish.

  3. Layer the cooked sausage and crumbled bacon over the hash browns.

  4. Sprinkle 1 1/2 cups of the shredded cheddar cheese on top.

  5. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, and ranch dressing mix until well combined. Pour this mixture evenly over the casserole.

  6. Top with the remaining 1/2 cup of cheddar cheese.

  7. Bake uncovered until the casserole is set and the cheese is melted and bubbly. 40–45 min

  8. Remove from the oven, let cool slightly, and serve warm.

Homemade Pizza Dough

105°FWarm water
5 minYeast proof
10 min–1 hrOptional rise

Ingredients tap to check off

Batch size

Make the dough

  1. Combine the warm water, yeast, and sugar in a large mixing bowl and stir to combine.

  2. Let the mixture sit until it becomes frothy and bubbles form. 5 min

  3. Gently stir in the olive oil.

  4. Add 2 cups of flour and the salt. Mix with a spatula until a ball begins to form (the dough will still be slightly sticky). Add more flour as needed to form a dough ball.

  5. Transfer to a floured surface and knead into a smooth dough, adding up to 1/2 cup extra flour if needed.

  6. Optional. Cover the bowl with a damp tea towel and let the dough rise. 10 min–1 hr

Tropical Hawaiian Chicken on a Sheet Pan

400°F200°C oven
25–30 minRoast
165°F75°C chicken

Ingredients tap to check off

Batch size

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).

  2. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, soy sauce, honey, garlic powder, ground ginger, chili flakes, salt, and pepper.

  3. Place the chicken breasts on a sheet pan. Drizzle the marinade over the chicken and turn to coat evenly.

  4. Add the pineapple chunks, red bell pepper, and onion slices to the pan around the chicken.

  5. Roast until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C) and the vegetables are tender. 25–30 min

  6. Garnish with fresh cilantro before serving.

  7. Serve the chicken with the roasted vegetables for a complete, easy meal.

Hawaiian Huli Huli Chicken Stack

2 hr+Marinate
MediumGrill heat
6–8 minPer side

Ingredients tap to check off

Batch size

Marinade

Chicken

Stack (optional layers)

Directions

  1. Make the Marinade: In a bowl, whisk together sesame oil, ginger, garlic, brown sugar, soy sauce, pineapple juice, and teriyaki sauce.

  2. Marinate the Chicken: Place chicken in a ziplock bag or shallow dish. Pour in the marinade, seal, and refrigerate for at least 2 hours (overnight for best flavor). 2 hr+

  3. Grill or Sear the Chicken: Remove chicken from the marinade (reserve a bit if you want to baste). Grill over medium heat for 6–8 minutes per side or until fully cooked. Alternatively, sear in a hot skillet until caramelized and cooked through. 6–8 min per side

  4. Optional – Grill Pineapple & Veggies: While the chicken cooks, grill pineapple slices and any veggies you'd like in your stack.

  5. Assemble the Stack: Start with a scoop of rice, layer with cabbage or lettuce, then add grilled chicken, pineapple, and any extras like avocado or bell pepper.

  6. Finish & Serve: Drizzle with extra teriyaki sauce, garnish with sesame seeds and green onions, and serve warm.

Baked Caesar Chicken with a Creamy Parmesan Pasta

375°F190°C
25–30 minBake
40 minCooking time

Ingredients tap to check off

Batch size

Directions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).

  2. Season the chicken breasts with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning.

  3. Coat the chicken with Caesar dressing and sprinkle with grated Parmesan cheese.

  4. Place the chicken on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and bake for 25–30 minutes, or until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (75°C). 25–30 min

  5. Meanwhile, cook the pasta according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.

  6. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat.

  7. Add garlic powder, heavy cream, and Parmesan cheese, stirring until the sauce is creamy and smooth.

  8. Add the cooked pasta to the skillet and toss to coat in the creamy Parmesan sauce.

  9. Serve the baked Caesar chicken over the creamy pasta, garnished with fresh parsley.
